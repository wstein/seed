== Known Gaps Against The Reference Runtimes

Seed targets a large, useful subset of the ANTLR runtime; it is not yet at
parity. The full status and the plan to close it live in
xref:manual:feature-parity.adoc[Feature Parity And Roadmap]. The one gap
that is *architectural* rather than merely unfinished:

* *Embedded actions* (`{...}`) cannot be run by the interpreter — it cannot
  execute the host-language code they compile to. They stay unaddressed by
  design: without typed contexts they have no attributes to act on, so
  meaningful support is folded into the codegen target, and post-parse
  computation is already served by the tree walk. (The related limit on
  *semantic predicates* `{...}?` is resolved: the decision is deferred to a
  caller-supplied evaluator — the parser's `Seed.ParserInterpreter.parse/4`
  `:sempred` / `Seed.Generated`'s `sempred/3` disambiguates alternatives, and
  the lexer's `Seed.Lexer.new/3` / `Seed.tokenize/3` `:sempred` gates rules.)

This is tracked in the roadmap with its scope and ordering.
